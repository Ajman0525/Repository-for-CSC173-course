## My kernel (Part 2c)
The numbers, and what it detects: The numbers of the emboss kernel I presented was [[-2, -1, 0], [-1, 1, 1], [0, 1, 2]]. The top-left numbers are negative (shadows) and the bottom-right numbers are positive (highlights), the kernel artificially darkens one side of an edge and brightens the other. This tricks the eye into seeing a 3D, stamped texture as if a light source is shining from the top-left corner.
